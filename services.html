Freight Services

Expedited, regional and long-haul trucking for shippers and brokers who can't afford delays.

01

Expedited Freight

When a shipment is time-critical, we dispatch quickly and run it direct from your dock to its destination — no terminals, no cross-docking, and no extra stops. You get updates throughout the trip and confirmation the moment it delivers.

  • Direct, non-stop transit
  • Fast dispatch
  • Line-down & production parts
  • Proactive updates
02

Same-Day & Next-Day

Urgent deliveries across Hampton Roads and the surrounding region — Newport News, Hampton, Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Williamsburg and Richmond — on deadlines measured in hours, not days.

  • Hot loads
  • Appointment deliveries
  • After-hours by arrangement
03

Regional Freight

Dependable runs throughout Virginia and the Mid-Atlantic, including Washington, DC, Maryland, North Carolina and Pennsylvania — ideal for distribution center deliveries and warehouse transfers.

  • Virginia & Mid-Atlantic
  • Warehouse transfers
  • DC deliveries
04

Long Haul & Interstate

Over-the-road freight from Virginia up and down the East Coast and across the continental United States, with trip planning that respects hours-of-service rules and your delivery appointments.

  • East Coast lanes
  • Nationwide
  • Check-calls & updates
05

Dedicated Runs

Freight that moves on a regular schedule? A dedicated arrangement gives you consistent capacity, familiar drivers and predictable pricing on your recurring lanes.

  • Recurring loads
  • Consistent pricing
  • Priority capacity

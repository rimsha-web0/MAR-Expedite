Who we are

Built for loads that can't wait.

MAR Expedite LLC is a Virginia trucking company headquartered in Newport News. Since 2024 we've focused on what shippers need most when the clock is running: a truck that's ready, a direct route, and a dispatcher who keeps you in the loop.

From urgent production parts to time-sensitive retail and port freight, we treat every shipment like it's the only one on the road.

  • Dispatch that answers and acts fast
  • Direct, point-to-point delivery — no terminals or transfers
  • Pickup, in-transit and delivery updates by text or email

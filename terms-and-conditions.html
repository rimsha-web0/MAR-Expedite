Program name & description

How to opt in

You may opt in by entering your mobile phone number on the contact form at marexpeditellc.com/contact.html and checking the box for the type of messages you wish to receive. The consent boxes are not pre-checked and are not required to submit the form. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your shipments and requests. Marketing messages are limited to up to 4 messages per month.

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by and payable to your mobile service provider. MAR Expedite LLC does not charge for text messages.

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text STOP to the number that sent you a message. After you send STOP, we will send you one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive text messages from us. To rejoin, opt in again through our Website form.

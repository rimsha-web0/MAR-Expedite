About MAR Expedite

A Hampton Roads trucking company built around speed, reliability and straight communication.

Our story

Founded in Virginia. Focused on the deadline.

MAR Expedite LLC was organized in the Commonwealth of Virginia in February 2024 by Michael Robinson and is headquartered in Newport News. The company was built on a simple idea: when freight is urgent, shippers deserve a carrier that moves just as urgently.

We specialize in expedited and time-sensitive freight, along with regional and long-haul runs up and down the East Coast. Every load gets the same treatment — a confirmed rate, a direct route, steady updates while it's moving, and proof of delivery as soon as it's signed.

Whether you're a manufacturer with a line-down emergency, a distributor with a tight appointment, or a broker who needs a dependable truck today, we're ready to help.
